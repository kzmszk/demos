# Source-asset notices

This source archive includes only the files listed in `ASSET_PUBLICATION.json`. Their exact SHA-256 values, source chains and omission reasons are recorded there. Original research ledgers are preserved with a dated source-publication addendum.

Photographer licenses are distinct from the rights in the stained-glass and sculptural works depicted. Existing rights reservations for Joan Vila-Grau, Etsuro Sotoo, Jaume Busquets and Josep Maria Subirachs remain. Publication of this project does not assert their endorsement or grant new underlying-artwork rights.

Retain source links, photographer attribution, the applicable CC license links and notices of modifications when redistributing these photos or derivatives. Share-alike photo adaptations retain the licenses stated below; do not apply a blanket software license over these assets.

Procedural stone images, weight-mask generation and the authored build code are original project work; this notice does not select a new general license for that work. Official reference-only imagery is excluded.

## Detailed records

- `ARTWORK_SOURCES.json`: original photograph, photographer, date, license, attribution and underlying-work distinctions.
- `MATERIAL_SOURCES.json`: original procedural materials, glazing interpretation and atlas derivation.
- `EXTERIOR_EVIDENCE.json`: sculpture source mapping, relief method, edge processing and Nativity color grading.
- `ASSET_PUBLICATION.json`: exact included and omitted asset paths, hashes, conditions and effects of omissions.
